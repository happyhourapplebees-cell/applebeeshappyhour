What Is Applebee’s Happy Hour?

Applebee’s Happy Hour refers to special offers available at participating restaurant locations during designated hours. Depending on the restaurant, these promotions may include selected appetizers, beer, cocktails, or other beverages at reduced prices.

It is a convenient option for customers who want to enjoy familiar favorites without paying full menu prices for every item. However, not every Applebee’s restaurant follows the same happy hour schedule or offers identical discounts.

Applebee’s Happy Hour Times

Applebee’s Happy Hour times depend on the location, franchise, and current promotions. A commonly reported schedule is an afternoon period followed by a late-night period, but these hours are not guaranteed at every restaurant.

Does Applebee’s Offer Half-Price Appetizers?

Some Applebee’s locations advertise late-night half-price appetizer specials. These promotions can include selected items such as boneless wings or mozzarella sticks, depending on the participating restaurant.

The discount generally applies only to eligible menu items and may be restricted to dine-in orders. Availability, timing, and exclusions can differ by location.

If you are visiting specifically for half-price appetizers, confirm the offer with your local Applebee’s before arriving.

Tips for Saving Money at Applebee’s Happy Hour

A little planning can help you make the most of your visit.
